You can evaluate Deployz for free, with no card and no time limit. You pay once you subscribe to install your application for customers: a $49/month platform fee, plus $19/month for each customer deployment while it runs for your customer.

Deployz fees cover the platform. AWS bills your customer directly for the resources their deployment uses. You don't resell infrastructure. See [Sizes and AWS cost](/share/sizes-and-cost).

## Free evaluation

Evaluation is free and never expires. It includes:

- Connecting applications, analysis, and configuration
- One [test deployment](/share/test-deployment) of your own application, per application
- Adding customers and building releases

A test deployment is never charged. AWS bills the account you install it into for the resources it uses.

## What you pay for

| Charge | Price | Starts | Stops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform | $49/month | When you subscribe | When you cancel your subscription |
| Each customer deployment | $19/month | When the deployment first becomes ready | When you remove it, as soon as Deployz accepts the removal. Paused while the deployment has failed or its connector is offline. |

Prices are in US dollars. For example, with five customer deployments running for your customers, you pay $49 + 5 × $19 = $144 a month.

### When the platform fee starts

The platform fee starts when you subscribe. Subscribe before your first customer confirms an install link or invitation, because a customer can confirm only while your subscription is active. You start the subscription from the **Invite customer** page. See [Start a subscription](#start-a-subscription). The first charge covers the platform fee only, because no customer deployment is live yet.

### When a customer deployment is charged

A customer deployment is charged only while it runs for your customer.

- **Start.** Deployz starts charging the first time the deployment reaches a verified ready state, with the application running and reachable over HTTPS. A deployment that's waiting for the customer, still installing, or failed its first install isn't charged.
- **Pause.** Deployz pauses the charge while the deployment has failed with nothing running for your customer, or while its Deployz connector is offline. The connector counts as offline after 15 minutes without a check-in, so a short gap doesn't pause billing. Deployz checks every 15 minutes, so a pause can take up to 15 minutes to apply.
- **Resume.** The charge resumes when the deployment is ready again, or when its connector reconnects and the application is healthy.
- **Failed updates.** A failed update that keeps the previous release running for your customer is still charged.
- **Stop.** The charge stops as soon as Deployz accepts your request to remove the deployment.

A test deployment is never charged.

While a deployment is paused, its page in the dashboard shows **Billing paused — not counted while it is failed or disconnected**.

After removal, retained data that you haven't deleted can keep costing money in your customer's AWS account, but it doesn't add to your Deployz bill. See [Remove a deployment](/operate/remove).

When the number of charged deployments changes during a billing period, Deployz updates your subscription with its billing partner, Paddle. The change is prorated for the rest of the period and applied right away, not at your next billing date. If Deployz can't reach Paddle at that moment, it retries later, so an update can take some time to appear.

### Included production deployments

Your organization might have a number of included production deployments. They're shared across all your applications and customers. You're charged $19/month only for each charged customer deployment beyond that number. Included deployments don't replace the platform fee. When your organization has any, the **Billing** page shows how many.

## Who can manage billing

Any member of your organization can start a subscription, open the billing portal, and update payment details. Billing isn't limited to the owner or admins. See [Roles](/account/organization-and-team#roles).

## Start a subscription

**Who:** you (the vendor), in the Deployz dashboard.

1. Open the **Invite customer** page. To get there, select **Invite customer** in **Customers**.
2. In **Start your subscription**, select **Start subscription**.
3. Enter your payment details in the checkout window and complete the payment.

When the payment goes through, the page shows "Payment received. Customers can now confirm installations." Your customers can now confirm install links and invitations.

The **Start your subscription** section appears on the **Invite customer** page while you're evaluating or after your subscription was canceled. If your payment failed or your subscription is paused, the page shows **Update your payment details** or **Resume your subscription** instead.

## Check your charges

**Who:** you, in the Deployz dashboard.

Select **Billing** in the sidebar.

- **During evaluation**, the page shows **Evaluation — free**, what's included, and what starts billing.
- **With a subscription**, **Current charges** shows your subscription status, the next billing date, the platform fee, the number of active, paused, included, and billed production deployments, the deployments by customer, and your monthly total. Paused deployments appear only when you have any.
- **After you cancel**, the page still shows **Current charges**, with a **Canceled** badge. A banner across the dashboard says **Your subscription has ended**.

If your last payment failed, a banner across the dashboard says **Your last payment did not go through**, with **Update payment details**. A paused subscription shows no banner.

## Manage payment details and invoices

**Who:** you, in the Deployz dashboard.

Deployz uses a billing partner, Paddle, for payments. Paddle handles your card, invoices and receipts, tax, and cancellation.

1. Select **Billing** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Manage billing**, select one of these:
   - **Open billing portal** to view invoices or cancel your subscription.
   - **Update payment details** to change your card.

## Subscription statuses

| Status | Meaning | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Evaluating** | You don't have a subscription yet. | Everything in the free evaluation. Start a subscription before customers install. |
| **Active** | Your subscription is paid and current. | Everything, including new customer deployments. |
| **Past due** | Your last payment failed. | Select **Update payment details** in the banner or on the **Billing** page. |
| **Paused** | Your subscription is paused. | Resume it in the billing portal. |
| **Canceled** | Your subscription has ended. | Start a new subscription from the **Invite customer** page. |

## Payment problems never stop running deployments

If a payment fails, or your subscription is paused or canceled, your existing deployments keep running. You can still deploy updates, roll back, restart, change configuration, and remove them.

Only new customer deployments wait. While your subscription isn't active, customers can't confirm install links or invitations. A customer who tries sees a message asking them to contact you. They can confirm again once your subscription is active.

## What your customers see

Your customers never see your Deployz pricing, subscription, or invoices. The only billing-related message they can see is the one above, when your subscription isn't active.
