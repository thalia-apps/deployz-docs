The estimate multiplies each baseline range by a factor for the AWS Region:

| Factor | Regions |
| --- | --- |
| 1.0 | US East (N. Virginia), US East (Ohio), US West (Oregon) |
| 1.1 | US West (N. California), Canada (Central), Europe (Ireland), Europe (Frankfurt), Europe (Stockholm), Asia Pacific (Mumbai), Asia Pacific (Singapore) |
| 1.2 | Europe (London), Europe (Paris), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), Asia Pacific (Seoul), Asia Pacific (Osaka) |
| 1.3 | Asia Pacific (Sydney) |
| 1.5 | South America (São Paulo) |

The factors are approximate. They don't reproduce each Region's exact AWS prices.
