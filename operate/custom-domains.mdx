A custom domain lets your customer reach their deployment at a hostname they own, such as `app.example.com`, instead of the address Deployz assigns. You add the domain in Deployz. Your customer creates two DNS records, and Deployz sets up HTTPS.

## How a custom domain works

- **You add and remove the domain.** Your customer can't add, change, or remove it. They can only create the DNS records and check their progress.
- **Your customer creates two CNAME records** in their own DNS: one that proves they own the domain, and one that routes traffic to the deployment. Deployz only reads their DNS. It never changes it.
- **The HTTPS certificate is created in your customer's AWS account**, in the deployment's Region, by the Deployz connector.
- **Traffic goes straight to your customer's AWS account.** The custom domain points directly at the load balancer in their account. The default Deployz address keeps working, and its traffic passes through the Deployz Cloudflare network first.
- **One custom domain per deployment.** A domain can be connected to only one deployment.

## Before you start

- The deployment must be running and show its **Application URL** on the detail page.
- Agree on the hostname with your customer. They need access to that domain's DNS settings.
- Use a subdomain, such as `app.example.com`. Deployz doesn't support:
  - Root domains, such as `example.com`
  - Wildcard domains, such as `*.example.com`
  - Full URLs: enter only the hostname, without `https://` or a path
  - IP addresses, or Deployz and AWS domains

## Add the domain

**Who:** you (the vendor), in the Deployz dashboard.

1. In **Deployments**, open the customer's deployment.
2. In the **Application URL** card, next to **Custom domain**, select **Add custom domain**. The deployment's install page opens. Because you're signed in to Deployz, you can manage the domain there.
3. Select **Set up custom domain**.
4. In **Domain**, enter the hostname, for example `app.example.com`.
5. Select **Add domain**.

Deployz requests a certificate. After a short wait, the card shows **Waiting for DNS** and lists two records:

| Record | Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Verify ownership** | `CNAME` | Proves your customer controls the domain, so the certificate can be issued. |
| **Route traffic** | `CNAME` | Sends visitors to the deployment's load balancer. |

Each record has a **Name** and a **Value** with a **Copy** button.

## Ask your customer to create the DNS records

**Who:** your customer, at their DNS provider.

Send your customer [Create custom domain DNS records](/customers/custom-domain-dns). They see the same records on their install page and can select **Check now** after creating them.

DNS changes can take some time to appear. Deployz checks automatically. While the domain shows **Waiting for DNS** or **Connecting**, you can also select **Check now** on the card. Deployz checks a domain at most once every 30 seconds, so if you select **Check now** again sooner, the card shows the latest result without a new check. When both records are found, Deployz finishes the certificate and sets up HTTPS.

When the card shows **Active** and **Available securely at:** with the `https://` address, the custom domain is ready. The **Application URL** on the detail page switches to the custom domain and shows **Also available at** with the default address.

## Domain statuses

| Status on the domain card | On the Application URL card | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Setting up** | **Waiting for domain setup** | Deployz is requesting the certificate. |
| **Waiting for DNS** | **Waiting for domain setup** | Deployz is waiting for your customer's two DNS records. |
| **Connecting** | **Connecting** | The domain is verified. Deployz is configuring HTTPS and checks that the domain answers over HTTPS. The domain becomes **Active** when it does. |
| **Active** | **Active** | The domain serves the deployment over HTTPS. |
| **Needs attention** | **Needs attention** | Setup stopped. Deployz doesn't retry it. |
| **Removing** | **Removing domain…** | Deployz is removing the domain. |

While a domain is waiting or connecting, the card can show the latest check result, such as **Verification record not found** or **Domain isn't pointing to this deployment**. These are normal until the records are in place.

If the card shows **This deployment isn't running**, Deployz has paused the domain checks because the deployment isn't running, for example because its first install failed or it's being removed. Setup continues when the deployment is running again.

When a domain shows **Needs attention**, the card explains why, for example **The HTTPS certificate couldn't be issued**. Remove the domain, fix the cause, then add it again. See [Fix HTTPS and custom domain problems](/troubleshooting/https-and-domains).

## Change or remove the domain

**Who:** you (the vendor). Open the domain card from **Manage custom domain** or **Check custom domain** in the **Application URL** card.

Before you remove a domain, check what happens:

- The domain stops routing to the deployment once removal finishes. The default address keeps working.
- Deployz doesn't delete your customer's DNS records. Ask your customer to delete the two records when they no longer need them.
- You can't edit a domain's hostname. To change it, remove the domain, then add the new one.

You can remove a domain in any status except **Removing**.

1. Select **Remove domain**.
2. In **Remove custom domain?**, select **Remove domain**.

While the domain shows **Removing**, **Check now** and **Remove domain** are unavailable. After removal, you can add a new domain.

Removing the deployment also removes its custom domain. See [Remove a deployment](/operate/remove).
